SPLIT GRAVITY

PRIVACY POLICY · LAST UPDATED 2026

Split Gravity is a single-player arcade game. There is no account, no login, and no way to enter a name, an email address or any other personal detail — the game never asks, so it cannot collect it.

Two third-party services do receive data, and this page explains exactly what and why.

Stored on your device only

  • High scores for each difficulty
  • Which difficulty and orb skin you last selected
  • Lifetime gates cleared, daily goal progress and streak
  • Whether audio is muted
  • Whether the ad-free version has been purchased

This never leaves your device and is deleted when the app is deleted. It is not backed up to any server we control.

Advertising — Google AdMob

The free version shows ads between runs, and an optional video ad you can choose to watch to continue a run. Google AdMob provides these and may use a device advertising identifier to select them.

  • On iOS you are asked for permission first, through Apple's system prompt. Decline and you still see ads, just less relevant ones.
  • In the EEA and the UK, a consent form appears before any ad is requested. Your choices can be changed at any time from Privacy Options on the game's start screen.
  • Buying the ad-free version stops all ad requests entirely.

Google's own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Analytics — Google Firebase

Anonymous gameplay events help us understand whether the game is fair and whether the ad frequency is tolerable. Specifically: which difficulty was played, how long a run lasted, the score, whether the run ended on an obstacle or the centre line, and whether an ad was shown or watched.

These events carry a random identifier generated on first launch. It is not your Apple ID, not your device ID, and it is regenerated if you reinstall.

Children

Split Gravity is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices

  • Deny tracking at the system prompt, or change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Use Privacy Options in the game to revisit consent (shown where the consent framework applies)
  • Buy the ad-free version to stop ad requests
  • Delete the app to erase everything stored on the device
